How I Think — Model 03

Behavioural Segmentation

Treating an audience as distinct groups by behaviour and eligibility, not one undifferentiated block.

What It Is

Behavioural segmentation groups an audience by what they actually do — active vs. dormant, adopted vs. not, engaged vs. lapsed — rather than by static demographic or firmographic data. Each behavioural group is stuck at a different point, and each needs a different message.

Why It Matters

A single message sent to an entire seller base always underperforms a message built for the specific friction point each segment is actually stuck on. Treating a dormant seller and an active power-seller the same way wastes the message on both.

How I Apply It

  1. Group the audience by observed behaviour, not static profile data.
  2. Identify the specific friction or motivation unique to each behavioural group.
  3. Build a distinct communication or intervention per segment.
  4. Track segment-level response, not just an aggregate metric.

In Practice — Sale Event Planner

Sale Event Planner was functional but underused — sellers saw it as an optional add-on rather than a revenue lever. Instead of one broad awareness campaign, I segmented sellers by actual usage behaviour and designed lifecycle campaigns — email, in-app messaging, Seller Central, webinars, WhatsApp — tuned to each segment's specific stage of adoption.

Result → 21% seller acquisition growth, 11% dormant reactivation, up to 34% engagement lift
